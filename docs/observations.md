The common reproduction command is:

The command intentionally exits `1`. Open `reports/evaluation-report.json`, locate

## OBS-01 - Citation quote does not match the cited policy

- **Recording:** R06
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Expected:** A citation must identify an eligible passage and quote actual text from
  that passage. P02 says the annual certification reimbursement limit is INR 25000.
- **Actual evidence:** The response cites P02, but its quotation says the limit is INR
  35000. That text is not present in P02.
- **User impact:** Users cannot reliably verify the answer from its citation and may be
  confused about whether the correct limit is INR 25000 or INR 35000.
- **Rationale:** The answer amount happens to be correct, but the supporting quotation is
  fabricated or incorrectly paired. This breaks evidence integrity without showing the
  same direct tenant or financial harm as the higher-severity defects.
- **Suspected cause, not proven:** Citation text may be generated instead of copied from
  the selected passage, or the quote and chunk ID may be paired incorrectly.
- **Next diagnostic step:** Replay R06 in a controlled environment and trace the selected
  chunk ID, retrieved passage text, and citation-building step. Confirm that quotations
  are copied from the authorized source passage rather than generated by the model.
